Behind the scenes: the data link between the stations

The two stations talk over an industrial protocol called TASE.2 (also known as ICCP). When you change something in Station A, the HMI makes a real protocol write, and Station B only updates from the messages that genuinely arrive. The values below are the named objects the stations exchange.
